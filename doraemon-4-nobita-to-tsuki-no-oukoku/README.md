DORAEMON 4: NOBITA AND THE MOON KINGDOM (ENGLISH) — VERSION 1.0

ABOUT THE GAME
Doraemon, Nobita and their friends set off on an adventure to the Moon Kingdom.
Explore, meet characters and use Doraemon's gadgets in this family adventure.

ABOUT THIS TRANSLATION
This is an AI-assisted English translation of Doraemon 4: Nobita to Tsuki no
Oukoku for the Super Nintendo. It translates the story, menus, location names
and password text while keeping the game's original characters and spirit.
Password screens use English letters and symbols.

Manual playtesting was carried out by Mr Sausage across portions of the game.
All text encountered during those play sessions appeared in English. This was
not a full-game completion or an exhaustive check of every screen and graphic.

PATCHING THE JAPANESE GAME
The patch for this release is the BPS file:
Doraemon 4 - Nobita and the Moon Kingdom (English) v1.0.bps

1. Make a backup of your Japanese game file.
2. Open Floating IPS (Flips) and choose "Apply Patch".
3. Select the BPS file, then select your clean Japanese Doraemon 4 ROM.
4. Choose a name and location for the new English ROM when prompted.
5. Open the new English ROM in your SNES emulator.

Use an unmodified, unheadered Japanese ROM. Do not apply the patch to an
already-patched ROM. If the patcher reports that the source does not match,
check the original ROM's checksums below.
